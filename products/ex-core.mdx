Ex-Core is Fable Fintech's comprehensive operations platform for money transfer operators (MTOs) and exchange houses. If your business currently runs on a patchwork of disconnected systems — one for teller cash, another for FX, a third for remittance, and a spreadsheet for compliance — Ex-Core brings all of that into a single, integrated environment. You get complete operational control across every branch, channel, and customer segment, without the reconciliation overhead that comes with fragmented infrastructure.

## Key Capabilities

- **Teller cash management** — Manage cash positions at the teller level across all your branches, with real-time visibility into denominations, buy/sell positions, and cash alerts
- **Branch FX operations** — Handle over-the-counter FX transactions with live rate feeds, margin configuration, and automatic position updating
- **Retail and corporate remittance** — Process outbound and inbound remittances for both walk-in retail customers and corporate account holders through the same platform
- **WPS (Wage Protection System) support** — Process employer salary disbursements in compliance with WPS requirements, with built-in reporting for regulatory submissions
- **Compliance and reporting** — Inbuilt AML screening, KYC management, and transaction monitoring ensure every transaction meets regulatory requirements
- **Complete operational control** — Branch managers and operations teams get real-time dashboards covering transaction volumes, cash positions, and exception queues

## Compliance

Ex-Core has compliance built into every transaction workflow — not bolted on as an afterthought. Before any payment is processed or funds are released, the transaction passes through:

- **AML screening** against configured watchlists and transaction behaviour rules
- **KYC verification** checks tied to your customer onboarding records
- **Sanctions screening** against OFAC, UN, EU, and local watchlists

Every compliance action is recorded in the audit trail. Transactions that trigger a review are held in a dedicated exception queue and processed under your configured **4-Eye** (dual approval) or **6-Eye** (triple approval) workflow before release.
