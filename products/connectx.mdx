ConnectX is Fable Fintech's next-generation platform, launched in 2025, designed to help exchange houses rapidly modernize and scale their operations for a digital-first world. While legacy systems lock exchange houses into slow, expensive upgrade cycles, ConnectX gives you a clean, API-native foundation that lets you expand into new corridors, onboard new partners, and serve customers digitally — without the technical debt of older infrastructure. If you run an exchange house and want to compete at the pace of the modern remittance market, ConnectX is built for you.

## Key Capabilities

- **Digital-first remittance operations** — Run your full remittance business on a platform designed for digital channels from day one, with mobile, web, and agent interfaces all supported natively
- **Rapid corridor expansion** — Add new sending and receiving corridors quickly using Fable's pre-integrated partner network, without building each connection individually
- **Pre-integrated compliance** — AML screening, KYC workflows, and sanctions checks are built into the platform and ready to configure from launch — no separate compliance system required
- **Modern API connectivity** — Connect your exchange house network to banks, aggregators, and payout partners through standardized, well-documented REST APIs that your technical team can work with directly

## Integration Ecosystem

ConnectX plugs directly into Fable Fintech's ecosystem of over 100 pre-tested integration partners, giving your exchange house instant access to collection rails, payout networks, compliance data providers, and FX rate feeds — all without managing individual partner certifications.

## Why ConnectX Now

The remittance industry is consolidating around digital-first operators who can offer customers real-time transfers, transparent pricing, and seamless mobile experiences. Exchange houses running on legacy platforms are losing ground to neobanks and digital MTOs that have built on modern infrastructure. ConnectX closes that gap — giving established exchange houses the technology foundation to compete, grow, and serve the next generation of customers without starting from scratch.

<Tip>
  ConnectX is particularly well-suited to exchange houses looking to expand their geographic footprint quickly. Because corridor connectivity is handled through Fable's pre-integrated network, you can launch in a new market in days rather than months.
</Tip>
