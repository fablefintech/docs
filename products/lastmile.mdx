LastMile handles the receiving side of bulk remittance — connecting your bank to the world's leading money transfer operators (MTOs) and managing every inbound payment flow from arrival to beneficiary credit. Rather than manually reconciling incoming transfers from multiple MTO partners, LastMile gives you a single integration point, automated pre-validation, and complete real-time visibility across all inbound corridors.

## Key Capabilities

- **Smart routing to beneficiary accounts** — Automatically map each inbound transaction to the correct account using configurable routing rules, reducing manual intervention and crediting errors
- **Pre-validation before crediting** — Every payment is validated against account data, compliance rules, and format requirements before funds are credited, eliminating costly post-credit reversals
- **Exception management** — Transactions that fail validation are quarantined and surfaced for review, with full context to resolve quickly
- **Real-time reconciliation** — Match inbound MTO transactions against expected payments in real time, keeping your nostro and correspondent accounts in sync
- **Partner MTO connectivity** — Connect to multiple MTO partners through a single LastMile integration rather than managing individual bilateral connections

## Compliance

Every inbound transaction processed by LastMile passes through automated sanctions screening and AML checks before funds are credited to a beneficiary account. Transactions that trigger compliance alerts are held for review under your bank's configured 4-Eye or 6-Eye approval workflow — ensuring no funds are credited until they have passed your compliance requirements.

<Note>
  Compliance checks run in parallel with pre-validation, so clean transactions are credited without delay while flagged transactions are routed for review automatically.
</Note>
