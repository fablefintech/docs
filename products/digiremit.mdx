DigiRemit is Fable Fintech's end-to-end P2P remittance product, enabling banks to offer both inward and outward international transfer capabilities to their retail customers. Whether your customers are receiving money from family abroad or sending funds to loved ones overseas, DigiRemit gives your bank the infrastructure to deliver a fast, transparent, and fully compliant experience — without building it from scratch.

## DigiRemit Inward

DigiRemit Inward lets your bank's customers receive funds sent from abroad, directly into their accounts. You get full corridor coverage, real-time status updates, and pre-credit compliance checks — all managed through a single integration.

**Key Capabilities**

- Receive money from friends and family abroad across supported corridors
- Multi-corridor support covering major sending regions globally
- Real-time notifications to beneficiaries on fund arrival
- Beneficiary management for recurring senders and receivers
- Automated compliance screening before funds are credited

## DigiRemit Outward

DigiRemit Outward enables your customers to send funds internationally with upfront rate transparency and multiple delivery options. Every transaction is tracked end-to-end and screened for compliance before it leaves your bank.

**Key Capabilities**

- Send funds internationally to 100+ corridors
- Transparent FX rates displayed upfront before transaction confirmation
- Multi-channel delivery: bank transfer, mobile wallet, and cash pickup
- Integrated compliance and AML screening on every outbound transfer
- Real-time transaction tracking from initiation to delivery

## Integrations

DigiRemit connects your bank to a wide ecosystem of collection and delivery partners so you can offer broad coverage without managing multiple bilateral relationships.

**Collection Partners**
- UPI (India)
- SWIFT (global bank transfers)
- NETS (Singapore)
- KNET (Kuwait)

**Delivery Networks**
- Mastercard Send
- TerraPay
- SWIFT gpi

<Tip>
  All integrations are pre-tested within Fable's partner ecosystem, reducing your time-to-market to as little as 30 days.
</Tip>
