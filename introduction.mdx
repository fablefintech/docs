Fable Fintech is a B2B payments infrastructure company purpose-built for the cross-border remittance and FX space. Whether you are a bank looking to modernize inward remittance processing, a money transfer operator (MTO) building a scalable exchange platform, or an SME needing seamless international payment rails, Fable Fintech provides the API-first, configurable infrastructure to power your operations — without starting from scratch.

## Product Suite

Fable Fintech's platform spans seven specialized products, each designed to address a distinct segment of the cross-border payments value chain. From peer-to-peer retail remittance to bulk FX settlement and SME payments, every product is pre-integrated with a network of 100+ banking, compliance, and payout partners.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="DigiRemit Inward" icon="arrow-down-to-line">
    A fully managed inward P2P remittance platform that enables banks and MTOs to receive cross-border transfers from global corridors with built-in compliance and reconciliation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DigiRemit Outward" icon="arrow-up-from-line">
    A robust outward P2P remittance solution for banks and exchange houses to originate cross-border transfers, with real-time FX rates and beneficiary management.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DigiForex" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    A bulk outbound FX payment engine designed for institutions handling high-volume corporate and treasury transfers across multiple currency corridors simultaneously.
  </Card>
  <Card title="LastMile" icon="flag-checkered">
    A bulk inbound payout platform that ensures reliable last-mile delivery of funds to end beneficiaries — including bank accounts, wallets, and cash pickup networks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="BizPay" icon="briefcase">
    A cross-border payments solution tailored for SMEs, enabling businesses to make and receive international payments with transparent FX rates and streamlined documentation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DigiBureau" icon="chart-candlestick">
    A comprehensive FX deal booking and management platform that helps institutions lock in rates, manage deal pipelines, and automate settlement workflows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ex-Core" icon="building-columns">
    A full-stack core platform for money transfer operators, providing end-to-end orchestration of remittance operations — from customer onboarding to payout settlement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ConnectX" icon="bolt">
    An exchange house acceleration solution that connects exchange houses to a curated network of banking partners, payment corridors, and compliance tools for faster market expansion.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
