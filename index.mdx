## Get Started in 4 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request Access">
    Contact Fable at [fablefintech.com](https://www.fablefintech.com/contact) to provision your sandbox environment and receive your API credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Use your API key in the `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header on every request. See the [Authentication guide](/authentication) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Your First Call">
    Follow the [Quick Start](/quickstart) to create a test transaction and verify your integration end-to-end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go Live">
    Review compliance requirements, configure your corridors and channels, and switch to production credentials when ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All API requests must be made over HTTPS. Sandbox and production environments use separate credentials and base URLs.
</Note>
