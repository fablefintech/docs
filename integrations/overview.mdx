## How Integration Configuration Works

Fable does not apply a one-size-fits-all integration stack. During onboarding, your implementation team works with Fable to select the right partners for each send and receive corridor you operate. Factors that influence your integration configuration include:

- **Corridor requirements** — The payment methods available in a given send or receive country
- **Licensing and regulatory obligations** — Which KYC and compliance providers are accepted by your regulators
- **Recipient preferences** — Whether your end customers prefer bank credit, mobile wallets, or cash
- **Volume and risk profile** — Tiered compliance checks based on transaction value and customer risk

Once configured, integrations are activated at the corridor level and can be updated over time as your business grows into new markets.

<Note>
  Integration availability depends on your corridors, licensing, and your agreement with Fable. Not all integrations listed in this documentation are available to every customer by default. Contact your Fable account manager to enable new integrations or swap existing providers.
</Note>
