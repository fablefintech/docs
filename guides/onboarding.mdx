Fable Fintech's deployment process is purpose-built for a 30-day go-live, giving your engineering, compliance, and operations teams a structured path from first API call to live transactions. This guide walks you through every stage of that journey — from submitting your initial access request to flipping your integration to production credentials.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request Access">
    Submit the contact form at [fablefintech.com/contact](https://fablefintech.com/contact), including the following details:

    - **Organization type** — bank, money transfer operator (MTO), exchange house, or SME
    - **Target corridors** — the send/receive country pairs you intend to operate (e.g., US→IN, GB→PH)
    - **Products of interest** — DigiRemit, DigiForex, LastMile, BizPay, DigiBureau, Ex-Core, or a combination

    A Fable account manager will review your request and reach out within one business day to schedule an onboarding call.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sandbox Provisioning">
    After the onboarding call, Fable provisions a dedicated sandbox environment for your organization. You will receive:

    - A sandbox **API key** scoped to your organization
    - The sandbox **base URL**: `https://api-sandbox.fablefintech.com/v1`
    - Access to this documentation portal and the full API Reference

    Typical turnaround for sandbox provisioning is **1–2 business days**. All requests to the sandbox must include your API key as a Bearer token in the `Authorization` header:

    ```bash
    Authorization: Bearer <your_sandbox_api_key>
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Integration Development">
    Build and test your integration entirely within the sandbox environment. The sandbox mirrors production behavior — including compliance checks, FX rate feeds, and delivery channel routing — so issues caught here will not surface in production.

    Resources available during this phase:

    - **API Reference** — full endpoint documentation with request/response schemas
    - **SDKs** — official client libraries for JavaScript, Python, and Java
    - **Guide pages** — including this one, covering remittance flows, FX deal booking, bulk processing, and webhooks

    Your assigned account manager provides technical support throughout the integration phase. Reach out via the shared Slack channel or email thread established during onboarding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compliance & KYC Setup">
    Before you can process live transactions, your compliance configuration must be established in collaboration with Fable's compliance team. This includes:

    - **KYC provider integration** — connect your identity verification provider or use Fable's built-in DigiBureau KYC module
    - **AML screening rules** — configure transaction monitoring thresholds, velocity rules, and alert routing
    - **Sanctions list management** — select the sanction lists to screen against (OFAC, UN, EU, HMT, and others)
    - **Approval workflows** — configure 4-Eye or 6-Eye approval chains for high-value or high-risk transactions

    Fable's compliance team will review your configuration and sign off before production access is granted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Corridor & Channel Configuration">
    Work with your Fable account manager to enable the specific corridors and delivery channels your customers will use. Configuration covers:

    - **Corridors** — each send/receive country pair (e.g., US→IN, GB→IN, AE→PH) must be explicitly enabled
    - **Delivery channels** — bank account transfer, mobile wallet, cash pickup, or LastMile agent network
    - **Currency pairs** — FX pairs available within each corridor
    - **Transaction limits** — per-transaction and daily limits per corridor, aligned with your regulatory obligations

    Corridor enablement may require additional regulatory documentation depending on your licensing and the jurisdictions involved.
  </Step>

  <Step title="UAT (User Acceptance Testing)">
    Once your integration is complete and compliance configuration is in place, you will run a formal User Acceptance Testing (UAT) phase in the sandbox. Fable provides a **UAT testing checklist** covering:

    - Successful end-to-end remittance flows across each enabled corridor
    - KYC approval and rejection scenarios
    - AML and sanctions screening outcomes
    - FX rate fetching, deal booking, and deal utilization
    - Webhook delivery and signature verification
    - Error handling and retry behavior for failed transactions

    UAT sign-off from both your team and Fable's technical team is required before production credentials are issued.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Production Go-Live">
    After UAT sign-off and compliance approval, Fable provisions your production credentials:

    - A production **API key**
    - The production **base URL**: `https://api.fablefintech.com/v1`

    To go live, update your environment configuration to use the production base URL and API key. No other code changes are required — your sandbox integration is fully compatible with production.

    <Note>
      Production credentials are issued **only after** your compliance review is complete and signed off by Fable's compliance team. Attempting to use sandbox credentials against the production endpoint will result in authentication errors.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Support

If you have technical questions at any stage of the onboarding process, contact your Fable account manager directly or submit a query via [fablefintech.com/contact](https://fablefintech.com/contact). Include your organization name, sandbox API key (never your production key), and a description of the issue for the fastest response.
